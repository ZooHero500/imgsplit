**Category:** Image Editing / Image Splitter / Design Tools

**Short Description (< 160 chars):**
Free, privacy-first online image splitter — split images into grids or custom pieces with pixel-perfect precision, 100% in your browser.

**Long Description:**
ImgSplit is a free, open-source online image splitting tool that runs entirely in your browser. No uploads, no servers, no sign-up required. Drag-and-drop split lines with snap alignment for pixel-perfect cuts. One-click grid splitting with 3x3, 1x3, and 2x2 presets for Instagram and social media. All image processing happens locally in your browser ensuring 100% privacy. Crops at original resolution with zero compression for lossless quality. Download all pieces as a ZIP or save individually. Supports PNG, JPG/JPEG, and WebP up to 20MB. Available in 5 languages: English, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, and Spanish. Responsive design works on desktop, tablet, and mobile. Perfect for e-commerce product images, social media grid posts, UI design slicing, and splitting long screenshots.
