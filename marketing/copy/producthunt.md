## Tagline

## Description (490 chars)
Free, open-source image splitter that runs 100% in your browser. No uploads, no servers, no sign-up.

Drag-and-drop split lines with snap alignment for pixel-perfect cuts. Create grids (3×3, 1×3, 2×2) for Instagram and social media. Split in half, into equal parts, for printing, or any custom layout.

Your images never leave your device. Works on desktop, tablet, and mobile. Supports PNG, JPG, WebP. Available in 5 languages. Open source (MIT) on GitHub.

Hey Product Hunt! 👋

I'm ZooHero, the maker of ImgSplit.

I built this because I kept running into the same problem: splitting an image into pieces always meant either opening Photoshop (expensive) or uploading to some random website (privacy risk).

ImgSplit solves both — it's completely free and your images never leave your browser. No server, no upload, no account.

**What makes it different:**
- Drag split lines exactly where you want them (not just fixed grids)
- Snap alignment for pixel-perfect precision
- Downloads as ZIP with one click
- Works on your phone too

**Use cases I've seen:**
- E-commerce sellers splitting product photos
- Instagram creators making carousel and grid posts
- Designers slicing mockups without Photoshop
- Students splitting long screenshots into pages

It's fully open source — check the code on GitHub if you're curious.
