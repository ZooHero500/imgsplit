## Long Description
ImgSplit is a free, open-source online image splitting tool that runs entirely in your browser. No uploads, no servers, no sign-up required.

**Key features:**
- **Drag-and-drop split lines** — Add up to 20 horizontal and 20 vertical lines with snap alignment for pixel-perfect cuts
- **Grid splitting** — One-click 3×3, 1×3, and 2×2 grid presets for Instagram and social media
- **100% privacy** — All image processing happens locally in your browser. Your images never leave your device
- **Lossless quality** — Crops at original resolution with zero compression
- **One-click download** — Download all pieces as a ZIP or save individually
- **Multi-format** — Supports PNG, JPG/JPEG, and WebP up to 20MB
- **Multi-language** — Available in English, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, and Spanish
- **Works everywhere** — Responsive design for desktop, tablet, and mobile

Perfect for e-commerce product images, social media grid posts, UI design slicing, and splitting long screenshots.
